Almost

We often neglect the idea of our own absence. How could it look, a place where we are not? We leave traces, cold seats, empty rooms full of unspoken words that echo. You could feel them.

This project starts there. In explicit non-existence.

Then the human ceases to absent itself. It forces its presence, squeezes onto the film, so small between cold buildings you could miss it. It is there, sitting small, weak, negligible. It pushes. It is in the center of the frame, walking, moving somewhere, still small and lost, not knowing of its watcher.

A hand is there, sudden and close. It could surprise you, warm and immediate. So big and prominent in the frame, and still somehow incomplete. Still lost. The figure is closer now. Dark, isolated, seeking. Then a crowd, cloudy and warm, close but unknown, unfamiliar. Everyone and no one.

And finally two faces. Close. Looking in the same direction, though absent to each other. Almost.
